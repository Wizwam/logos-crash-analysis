Hi Uncle Martin,

This one is just you and me. It is not the Logos support thread, so Tommy will not see it.

The watchers on your MacBook are doing their job. Keep using Logos the normal way tonight and tomorrow: dock, undock, walk around the house. Leave the lid open so the watcher stays awake. If you can, leave Logos running when you plug or unplug the dock. That is how we catch the crash.

If you feel like trying a few extra things, do one at a time. No rush. Normal use is still the most useful test.

1. At the desk, turn Wi-Fi off. Stay on the dock. Use Logos for a bit. Then unplug only the Ethernet cable (leave the dock and the screens plugged in).

2. Stay docked with both Wi-Fi and Ethernet on. Open System Settings → Network and drag Wi-Fi above Ethernet, or the other way around. You do not need to unplug anything.

3. After the laptop wakes from sleep, wait about two minutes. Then in Logos use Close All. If the window freezes, leave it 30 to 45 seconds and take two screenshots of the whole window (same view both times). Do not force-quit until you have those.

4. Optional: quit Logos, hold the Command key while you open it so you get a blank layout, then try unplugging the dock once.

If it freezes, the window stays but stops updating. If it crashes, the window vanishes. A full-window screenshot of either is gold. Skip any screen that shows a password or a login page.
